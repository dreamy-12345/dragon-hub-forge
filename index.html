Fire spinning and flow arts come with real risks: burns, fire, injury, and damage to people and property. By making, using or performing with any prop designed here, you take part entirely at your own risk.

These are designs and guidance, not tested or certified products. We take no liability for these props, for how they are made, or for how they are used. Check every prop before every burn, train with experienced spinners, and always have a safety person and fire blanket ready.
